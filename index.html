About the game

What is the Canuckle word game?

Canuckle is a daily guessing game built around Canada. Every answer is a five-letter word tied to the country – from wildlife on our coins to the snacks in our sugar shacks. You get six guesses, and after each one the tiles tell you which letters are right, which are close and which you can forget about.

One shared puzzle per day means everyone in the country is chasing the same word, which makes comparing results with friends and family part of the fun.

Scoring

How repeated letters are scored

Double letters trip up new players more than anything else. The rule is simple: each letter in the answer can only light up one tile.

Answer: BISON

SASSY

Guessing SASSY: the S in the middle is red. The other two S tiles turn grey, because BISON contains only one S and it is already matched.

Answer: GOOSE

OOMPH

Guessing OOMPH: both O tiles light up because GOOSE really has two Os – one is in the right spot (red), the other belongs elsewhere (gold).

Strategy

A three-guess routine that works

  1. Guess 1 – cover vowels. Start with a word rich in vowels and common letters, such as CANOE or AROSE.
  2. Guess 2 – new consonants. Use five fresh letters you have not tried yet, for example LIGHT or DUMPY, even if you already have a gold tile.
  3. Guess 3 – think theme. With most letters known, ask yourself: animal, food, place or slang? The Canadian theme narrows the list fast.

Common mistakes

  • Re-using grey letters in later guesses.
  • Putting a gold letter back in the same spot.
  • Forgetting that words like GEESE or TAFFY repeat a letter.
  • Using the first-letter hint too early.

FAQ

Canuckle word game FAQ

What is the Canuckle word game?

Canuckle is a daily word puzzle in which you guess a five-letter word with a Canadian connection in six tries. Coloured tiles show which letters are correct after every guess.

What do the colours mean in Canuckle?

Red means the letter is in the word and in the right spot, gold means it is in the word but in a different spot, and grey means the letter is not in the word.

How are repeated letters scored?

Each letter in the answer can only colour one tile. If you guess a letter twice but the answer contains it once, only one tile turns red or gold and the other turns grey.

Can I play more than one Canuckle a day?

Yes. After the daily word, switch to practice mode for unlimited extra rounds. Practice rounds do not affect your daily streak.

Do hints break my streak?

No. Hints never reset your streak, but your shared result shows how many hints you used.

Does Canuckle work on my phone?

Yes. The game runs in any modern mobile browser with an on-screen keyboard, and no app or sign-up is needed.
